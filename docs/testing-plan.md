**No test has been run yet.** Results will be added here.

## Set-up
- Same produce (e.g. tomato or onion slices of equal thickness) in the dryer and as an open-sun control.
- Clear days, approximately 9:00–17:00.
- Readings every hour.

## Measurements

| Quantity | Instrument | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Sample mass | Digital balance | Selected trays on each level + control |
| Air temperature | Digital thermometer / thermocouples | Each tray level inside, ambient outside |
| Relative humidity | Hygrometer | Inside and outside |
| Solar radiation | Solar power meter (W/m²) | Next to the dryer |
| Rack speed | Stopwatch / tachometer | Rack |
| Motor current and voltage | Multimeter | Motor supply |

## Calculations
- Moisture content (wet basis): $M_{wb} = \frac{W_i - W_d}{W_i} \times 100\%$
- Drying rate: $DR = \frac{W_t - W_{t+\Delta t}}{\Delta t}$
- Temperature rise: $\Delta T = T_{inside} - T_{ambient}$
- Uniformity: spread of final moisture content between trays, with and without rotation.
