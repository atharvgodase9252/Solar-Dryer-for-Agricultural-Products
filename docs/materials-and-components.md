# Materials and components

| Component | Material / type | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Base stand | Mild-steel square tube, welded | Supports the tabletop and drive |
| Tabletop | Laminated board, round, centre hole | Platform for rack and dome; caster running surface |
| Rotating rack | Mild-steel square tube | Carries the trays at several levels |
| Trays | Galvanised iron (GI) sheet, folded sector shape | Hold the produce |
| Casters | Swivel casters, bolted to arm ends | Carry rack load on the tabletop |
| Dome | 7 curved transparent panels (2.5 mm, CAD) with metal strip ribs, screwed to tabletop edge; Ø1300 mm × 450 mm | Traps solar heat, protects produce |
| Ventilation fan | Small axial fan on a round mounting ring in the Ø250 mm dome top opening | Removes warm, moist air |
| Motor | Geared DC motor | Rotates the rack |
| Transmission | Roller chain and sprockets (small on motor, large on central shaft) | Speed reduction / torque to shaft |
| Shaft support | Ball bearing | Centres and supports the shaft |
| Bracket | Orange plastic mounting bracket (drilled) | Mounting |
| Fasteners | Bolts, nuts, self-tapping screws | Assembly |

Quantities and ratings are to be added.
