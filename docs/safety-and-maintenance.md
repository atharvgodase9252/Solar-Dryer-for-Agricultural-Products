# Safety and maintenance

## Safety
- Keep hands clear of the sprocket and chain drive when the motor is powered; fit a guard.
- Secure motor wiring away from the moving rack and away from water.
- Check casters, bearing and welds before each run; the rack must not rub the dome.
- Galvanised trays: use food-grade liners if the coating is damaged; do not let acidic produce stand on bare damaged zinc.
- Lift the dome with two people.

## Maintenance
- Clean trays and the inside of the dome after every batch.
- Clean the dome with mild soap and a soft cloth only; avoid solvents.
- Lubricate the chain and bearing periodically; check chain tension.
- Inspect steelwork for rust and repaint where needed.
