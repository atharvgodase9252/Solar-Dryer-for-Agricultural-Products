# Project overview

## Problem
Most small farmers dry fruit, vegetables, grain and spices in open sun, on the ground or on rooftops. It is free, but the produce is exposed to dust, insects, birds, animals and sudden rain; drying is slow and uneven, and quality and market price drop. Electric or fuel dryers are too expensive to buy and run for small farms. Fixed box-type solar dryers help, but trays in sunnier positions dry faster, so the trays must be moved by hand.

## Our solution
A **rotary solar dryer**: multi-tier galvanised trays on a steel rack inside a transparent dome. A geared motor turns the rack slowly so every tray gets the same sunlight.

## Objectives
1. Protect produce from contamination while drying.
2. Raise the air temperature around the produce using solar heat only.
3. Give all trays uniform exposure by rotating the rack.
4. Use low-cost, locally available materials (MS square tube, GI sheet, transparent sheet, standard casters and sprockets).
5. Measure drying performance against open-sun drying.

## Comparison

| Approach | Potential advantages | Limitations |
|---|---|---|
| Open-sun drying | No equipment cost | Contamination, slow, uneven, weather-dependent |
| Electric / fuel dryer | Fast, controlled | High purchase and running cost |
| Fixed box solar dryer | Cheap, protected | Uneven exposure, manual tray rotation |
| **Our rotary solar dryer** | Protected, solar heated, rotating for uniform exposure | Performance not yet measured |
