READY-MADE SOLIDWORKS DOME ASSEMBLY

Open Solar_Dome_7_Panel_Assembly.step in SolidWorks.
It contains 7 separate curved panel components positioned around the 1300 mm diameter base.
Base diameter: 1300 mm
Dome height: 450 mm
Top opening diameter: 250 mm
Panel thickness: 2.5 mm
Number of panels: 7

The assembly is supplied as STEP because native .SLDASM/.SLDPRT files require the SolidWorks application itself to generate them. SolidWorks can open the STEP assembly and preserve the separate components.
